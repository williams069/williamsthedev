## Important before accepting real orders

- Prices: Update the example prices in the `PRODUCTS` and `FABRICS` arrays near the bottom of the file.
- Images: Replace the image URLs with real photographs of your Nigerian tailoring work for the best results.
- WhatsApp: The store number is configured as `+2349061284550`. The forms open WhatsApp with a prefilled message; customers must still press Send.
- Payments and security: This is a front-end website, not a full e-commerce backend. Orders are sent to WhatsApp; it does not independently process payments, securely store customer accounts, or manage inventory.
- Social media: Replace the sample Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links with your actual business profiles.
